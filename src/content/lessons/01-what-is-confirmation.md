Confirmation is one of the seven sacraments of the Catholic Church and, together with Baptism and the Eucharist, it is one of the three **Sacraments of Initiation**. Through Confirmation, baptized Christians are more perfectly bound to the Church and are enriched with a special strength of the Holy Spirit. Because of this gift, they are, as true witnesses of Christ, more strictly obliged to spread and defend the faith by word and deed.

The word *confirmation* comes from the Latin *confirmare*, which means "to strengthen." At Baptism, we receive the gift of divine life and become children of God. At Confirmation, that gift is deepened — the Holy Spirit comes to us in a new and personal way to help us live out our faith as adult Catholics.

Jesus promised His disciples that He would send them "another Advocate" (John 14:16), the Holy Spirit, to be with them forever. That promise was fulfilled at **Pentecost**, when the Holy Spirit descended on the Apostles in tongues of fire, giving them the courage and clarity to proclaim the Gospel. Confirmation is our personal Pentecost — the same Spirit that filled the Apostles comes to fill us.

## Key Points

- Confirmation is one of the three **Sacraments of Initiation**, alongside Baptism and the Eucharist
- It **completes** the grace of Baptism and strengthens the gifts of the Holy Spirit in us
- The sacrament is administered through the **anointing with Sacred Chrism** and the words *"Be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit"*
- A Confirmation sponsor accompanies the candidate and helps them live out their faith
- Each candidate chooses a **Confirmation name** — typically the name of a saint who inspires them

## What Happens During the Sacrament?

The ordinary minister of Confirmation is a **bishop**. During the Rite of Confirmation:

1. The candidates are presented to the bishop
2. The bishop extends his hands and prays for the outpouring of the Holy Spirit
3. Each candidate, with their sponsor, approaches the bishop
4. The bishop anoints the candidate's forehead with Sacred Chrism in the sign of the cross
5. He says: *"[Name], be sealed with the Gift of the Holy Spirit"*
6. The candidate responds: *"Amen"*
7. The bishop says: *"Peace be with you"*
8. The candidate responds: *"And with your spirit"*

This anointing leaves an indelible spiritual mark — a *character* — on the soul. Like Baptism, Confirmation can only be received once.
